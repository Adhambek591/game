Last updated: 19 November 2026. Developer: Nukus Animation LLC (Nukus, Karakalpakstan, Uzbekistan).

English

The game does not ask for, collect or send any personal information. Your progress, settings and coins are stored only on your device (local storage) and you can delete them by clearing the app data. The game may show optional rewarded advertisements from an advertising partner (Google AdMob or Yandex Advertising Network). These partners may use device identifiers to show and measure ads according to their own privacy policies. In-app purchases, if any, are processed by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details. The game is not directed at children under 13 to collect data, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone. Contact: through the developer contact listed on the Google Play page of the game.
