## Player moveset
- Fast attack (bA / J / Space): 4-hit combo — diagonal slash, horizontal slash, heavier chop (x1.2), spin finisher (x1.6, 250° arc, crit + knockdown). Hold bA = strong attack (old), counter after parry/perfect dodge = finisher hit.
- Strong attack: NEW button #bS (E/U). Chains cleave -> sweep -> launcher (tap again after the hit); after light hits it picks sweep/launcher. Costs 20 stamina (hold-A heavy too). Breaks guards (gb).
- Dodge #bD (K/Shift): short sidestep 3.3 m, i-frames .3 s, keeps facing (faces the lock target), 15 stamina; no stick = backstep. Double tap bD (or R) = ROLL 5.4 m, i-frames .5 s, 28 stamina (a dodge can be upgraded to a roll within .3 s). Swipe on the right half of the screen = dodge in that direction, double tap on empty screen = roll. Perfect dodge (old) still works for both.
- Block/parry: NEW #bB (Q/I), hold. Frontal melee x0.08 (bosses x0.3), costs stamina (0.75 x dmg, bosses 1.25 x), arrows blocked for 5 stamina; guard breaks at 0 stamina (0.7 s stun). Pressing guard < 0.3 s before a melee hit = PARRY: no damage, slow-mo, sparks, attacker staggered 1.5 s (bosses lose 40% poise), counter window 1.7 s with "⚔ Counter" prompt above the attack button (next attack = teleport finisher, x2.6). Red (unblockable) attacks break the guard; AoE (op without mel/proj) is never blockable.
- Jump: NEW #bJ (F). 0.62 s, 1.35 m, legs tucked (procedural). Jumps over low attacks (shockwaves, sweeps: op.low). Attack in the air = plunge (aims at the nearest enemy, AoE crit knockdown on landing); strong in the air = bigger plunge. With hard lock and no stick it leaps at the target.
- Lock-on: NEW #bL (Tab/L; X releases). Tap = lock / cycle targets left->right, hold > .45 s = release. Gold cone over the target, ring, camera pulls 28% toward it, strafing (faces the target, walk speed) within 6.5 m, auto-switches to the nearest enemy when the target dies.
- Stamina: yellow #sbar under the HP bar (inside #pbars), 100, regen 32/s after .65 s (20/s from empty), none while guarding; turns red when an action is refused.
- Executions: light attack on an ordinary/elite enemy at <32%/<25% HP (or broken poise <45%): 25% / 50% / 90% chance, max once per 7 s: slow-mo, camera zooms to 50% and centres on the pair, slash + chop, kill.
- Signs: Guloyim's amulet powers stay on b1/b2/b5/b8 with their painted icons (repositioned in the new cluster).
